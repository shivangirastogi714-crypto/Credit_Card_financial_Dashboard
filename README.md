# Credit_Card_Financial_Dashboard
Power BI Dashboard

## Project Objective
To develop a comprehensive weekly credit card dashboard that provides real-time insights into key performance metrics and trends, enabling stakeholders to monitor and analyze credit card operations effectively.

## Dataset
Financial Dataset (Credit Card Transactions and Customer Data)

## Steps
1. **Data Preparation:** Cleaned and transformed raw transaction and customer datasets for analysis.
2. **Data Modeling & DAX:** Built data models and created custom DAX measures to calculate key metrics like Revenue, Transaction Amount, Interest, and WoW (Week-over-Week) growth.
3. **Dashboard Creation:** Designed interactive Power BI reports to visualize weekly trends and Year-to-Date (YTD) performance.

## Insights

### WoW (Week-over-Week) Change – Week 53 (31st Dec)
* **Revenue Growth:** Overall revenue increased by **28.8%** compared to the previous week.
* **Transaction & Customer Growth:** Total transaction amount, transaction count, and customer count also showed steady week-over-week growth.

### Overview YTD (Year-to-Date)
* **Overall Revenue:** **57M**
* **Total Interest:** **8M**
* **Total Transaction Amount:** **46M**
* **Gender Contribution:** Male customers contributed more to revenue (**31M**) compared to female customers (**26M**).
* **Card Category:** **Blue & Silver** credit cards contribute to **93%** of overall transactions.
* **Top Performing States:** **TX, NY, and CA** contribute to **68%** of the overall business.
* **Activation Rate:** Overall activation rate is **57.5%**.
* **Delinquent Rate:** Overall delinquent rate is **6.06%**.

## Action Items
* **Focus on Key States:** Increase targeted marketing campaigns in **TX, NY, and CA** to maximize revenue from top-performing regions.
* **Card Upgrade Offers:** Introduce upgrade incentives for **Blue and Silver** cardholders to transition them to higher-tier cards.
* **Improve Activation Rate:** Launch onboarding rewards or cashback offers to boost the **57.5%** activation rate among new cardholders.
* **Reduce Delinquency:** Implement automated payment reminders and stricter credit risk assessments to lower the **6.06%** delinquent rate.
